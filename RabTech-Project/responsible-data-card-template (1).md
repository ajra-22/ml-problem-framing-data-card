# Responsible Data Card

## Dataset purpose
State the decision this dataset may support and the decisions it must not make.

## Provenance and permission
Document how the data was created, who may use it, and whether consent or licensing restrictions apply.

## Population and representation
Describe represented groups, missing groups, sampling limitations, and known imbalance.

## Features and target
Explain every feature, the target label, possible leakage, and sensitive proxies.

## Quality checks
Record missingness, duplicates, outliers, class balance, and train/test separation.

## Risks and safeguards
List bias, privacy, misuse, false-positive, and false-negative risks with mitigations.

## Intended evaluation
Name baseline, performance, calibration, fairness, and error-analysis measures before training.
